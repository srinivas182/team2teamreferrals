# Brand kit at scale (Sprint 2c)

One national brand across 46 industries. Everything sits inside the cobalt (#2451F5, sender) and orange (#FF7A1A, receiver) relay brand; industries are told apart by a family accent, never by a new brand.

|---|---|---|
| Real estate and property | #2451F5 | Real estate, commercial, property management, title and escrow, custom builders, land, ranch and farm |

| Events, travel and lifestyle | #BE185D | Travel, weddings and events, fitness, funeral pre-planning, timeshare |

Accents are used for tiles, emblems and small highlights only. Buttons, links and focus states always stay cobalt; the brand reads the same in every industry.

## Industry tiles

Rounded square (radius 25% of size), family accent at 12–14% opacity behind a single-weight glyph in the accent colour, and the cobalt-to-orange relay dot in the bottom-right corner. Sizes: 26, 32, 44, 64 px. Glyphs come from Lucide (ISC licence) for one consistent stroke weight.

## Family emblems

Eight custom-drawn emblems (48 px grid, 2.2 px rounded strokes) sitting on the relay arc. Used on industry hubs, the brand kit, marketing and app store artwork.

## Share cards

1200×630 (LinkedIn, Facebook, X), with 1080×1080 and 1080×1920 variants using the same layout: network name, "We claimed {city}.", "Send us your {clients} in {city}. Verified, tracked, trusted.", relay arc, founding-member pill in the family accent, domain. Headline size steps down for long city names so text never overflows.

## Motion

- **Relay:** every handoff animates as the cobalt-to-orange arc, 1.4 s, ease-out, once.
- **Map fill:** cities light up in sequence on load.
- **Count up:** key numbers count up once on first view, 1.2 s.
- **Pop:** confirmations scale in from 60% over 320 ms.
- All motion is skipped when the user prefers reduced motion.

## Photography

Real pros at work in their own city (on a roof, at a kitchen table, in a showroom), natural light, the city recognisable where possible, diverse ages and backgrounds, one subject with space for text, crops at 16:9, 1:1 and 4:5. Licensed images only, with model releases stored alongside. No stock handshakes.

## Illustration

Flat geometric scenes on the relay arc: two places, one handoff. Navy line work, family-accent fills, no gradients except the arc.
